# Voice pack: generic operator

Copy this file, rename it, and edit it to make content sound like you. Point /xray-content at your copy.
Keep private packs (with real client names or unpublished numbers) out of public repos.

## Who is talking
A founder or GTM lead who builds systems and shares what they find. Peer to peer, not guru to student.

## Rules
- Line one carries the most surprising number from the run.
- Short sentences. One idea per line on X.
- Name the tool, the folder, the count. Specific beats clever.
- Say what is SEEN and what is an estimate.
- Respectful about the company being X-rayed: the point is what is public and what it teaches.
- End with one plain CTA, or none.

## Never
- Em dashes.
- Hype words: game-changer, revolutionary, insane (unless quoted), secret, hack, unlock, leverage, delve.
- "Here's the thing", "Let that sink in", "Most people don't know".
- Invented numbers, customers or quotes.

## Formats that work
- Hook number + 3-5 findings + the play + CTA.
- "We ran it on ourselves first" (self-scan) posts.
- Side-by-side comparisons of 2-3 well-known companies.
