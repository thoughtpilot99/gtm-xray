# Disclaimer

GTM X-Ray reads information that companies publish to everyone: public web pages, public job-board feeds,
public DNS records, public sitemaps, the Internet Archive, and public ad libraries. It does not log in,
bypass access controls, submit forms, call private APIs, or collect personal data beyond what a company
publishes about its own roles and staff pages.

- Use it for lawful competitive research, market analysis, sales preparation and to audit your own exposure.
- Respect each site's terms of service and robots rules. Keep request volumes polite (the defaults are).
- Findings marked INFERRED or ESTIMATE are interpretations, not facts. Check before you act on them or publish them.
- Do not use the output to harass individuals, to impersonate a company, or for anything unlawful.
- HAR analysis is for captures of your own sessions only.
- Company names you analyze are only used to show what their public data reveals.
